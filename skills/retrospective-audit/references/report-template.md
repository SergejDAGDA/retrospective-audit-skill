## Recommendations

### Keep

Use for rules that worked as intended or protected against meaningful risk.

### Change

Use for rules that are directionally right but need sharper wording, ordering, or
scope.

### Add

Use for missing rules, checks, stop-lines, examples, or output requirements that
would have prevented repeated issues.

### Remove

Use for stale, unused, misleading, or unnecessarily costly instructions.

### Not enough data

Use for plausible improvements without enough historical evidence.

## Patch plan

List proposed file/section changes. Do not apply them without separate approval.
